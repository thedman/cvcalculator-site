OPTrust Commuted Value: Your Pension Options When You Leave

Leaving an OPTrust-covered job creates a pension decision, not just an employment change. You may retain a pension, transfer its commuted value or move pension service to another plan. Which choices are available depends on your benefits, age and circumstances.

This guide addresses the OPSEU Pension Plan's primary schedule. CVCalculator can help you explore an estimated value and scenarios; it does not replace your official OPTrust statement or determine your eligibility.

By CVCalculator / Published October 3, 2026 / Educational information

Which OPTrust pension do you have?

OPTrust administers the OPSEU Pension Plan, including a separate schedule called OPTrust Select. Select has different benefit provisions. Check your statement before applying a rule from this guide: primary-schedule pension features must not be assumed to apply to Select. Select members should use the official Select guide together with current plan terms and OPTrust's advice.

What happens to your OPTrust pension when you leave?

Start with your termination statement. OPTrust's termination documentation identifies several possible paths, depending on your circumstances. A change of position or employer can also involve special membership-transfer arrangements.

Keep a deferred pension

If entitled to a deferred pension, you leave the benefit with OPTrust for future payment. For the standard primary-schedule case, it is payable without early-age reduction at 65, with an earlier reduced start possible from 55. Qualifying grow-in rights can alter early-retirement entitlement. Deferring does not mean continuing to earn service after employment ends.

Transfer a commuted value, if eligible

A CV is a present-value settlement of pension benefits, not a withdrawal of an employer-matched savings balance. Its destination and tax treatment matter as much as the headline amount.

Transfer pension service or start a pension

An applicable agreement may allow a service transfer to your new plan. Members entitled to an immediate pension have different choices from a member with only deferred entitlement. Ask OPTrust which branch applies before comparing numbers. Our leaving-employer guide explains the broader process.

Check any separate refund

The member handbook describes the post-1986 contribution test: contributions with interest above half the corresponding pension CV produce an excess-contribution refund. This is separate from cash arising because a CV exceeds a tax-transfer limit. Do not assume all contributions are refundable.

Can you transfer your OPTrust commuted value?

For the ordinary deferred-pension route, plan text sections 10.5 and 10.8 require the relevant deferred entitlement and an election before 55. A member already entitled to an immediate unreduced pension, including Factor 90, is not in that ordinary deferred category. Disability-pension receipt also matters. Being under 55 does not establish eligibility by itself.

An eligible deferred member can elect later before the applicable cutoff. This is distinct from how long a particular quote remains valid. Legacy pre-1966 service under section 10.6 has different rules, including an earlier CV cutoff; ask OPTrust if that provision appears in your records.

Grow-in rights and involuntary termination

Qualifying involuntary termination can preserve access to an early unreduced pension at a future date. OPTrust's grow-in guidance specifies employment, age/service and certification conditions. It also confirms that an eligible member under 55 can choose a CV including the additional grow-in value. Future grow-in entitlement is different from already qualifying for an immediate unreduced pension.

Cases requiring individual confirmation

Divestment and successor employment can change when termination rights arise. A direct move into Ontario's PSPP without an employment break can engage special transfer rules. Small-pension commutation and shortened-life payments have their own provisions, so the ordinary cutoff is not a statement that no lump-sum payment is ever possible after 55.

Confirm before acting: Ask OPTrust to identify your entitlement, the applicable election cutoff and any special provisions in writing. A birthday, employment change and quote expiry are different events.

What does the commuted value represent?

It expresses future pension payments as a lump sum today. Benefit details and actuarial assumptions determine the result; your contributions alone do not. OPTrust's pension-options explanation identifies service, average salary, member and spouse ages, inflation protection and survivor benefits among the relevant factors.

Applicable actuarial standards include CIA section 3500. Those standards do not turn every pension into an identical calculation. See our calculator methodology guide for a concise explanation of the estimate inputs.

What might you give up by taking the commuted value?

A completed transfer settles the corresponding pension entitlement. Consider the income and protections you would otherwise retain, not only the balance arriving at a financial institution.

  • Lifetime income: a retained pension pays under plan terms; investing transferred money introduces responsibility for returns, costs and making the money last.
  • Inflation protection: the primary deferred pension has plan indexation provisions. Preserving that protection yourself is a different task from holding a large initial balance.
  • Survivor protection: pension survivor provisions depend on eligibility and personal circumstances. Your comparison should include the household, not only your own projected income.
  • Eligible insured benefits: OPTrust states that a CV transfer forfeits applicable government post-retirement insured coverage, and its cost is not included in the CV. Not every member qualifies. Confirm coverage separately with OPTrust and your employer.

The insured-benefits distinction is explained in OPTrust's termination manual. A calculator balance cannot replace checking which benefits apply to you.

Where can the transferred money go?

Permitted routes include a prescribed locked-in arrangement such as a LIRA, a pension plan that accepts the transfer, or a qualifying deferred life annuity. A LIF has additional age and regulatory conditions; it is not an unrestricted cash account. FSRA explains these destinations.

For relevant transfers to an RRSP-type arrangement or money-purchase provision, Income Tax Regulations section 8517 sets the prescribed tax-deferred amount. It is not simply a percentage of the CV. Read our LIRA / Maximum Transfer Value explanation for the general framework.

An amount above the applicable limit paid in cash is taxable, with withholding at payment. Withholding is not necessarily your final tax bill. Available RRSP room may permit separate relief, subject to your circumstances. A DB-to-DB service transfer has different tax rules; do not apply the LIRA limit to every destination. See commuted-value tax treatment and CRA's transfer guidance.

Why can your OPTrust commuted value change?

Compare quotes only after identifying their valuation dates and benefit inputs. All else equal, higher discount rates generally lower the present value of a future income stream. Age, timing and updated pension information can also change a calculation; this is not a prediction of your next quote. Our CIA-rate guide explains rate sensitivity.

OPTrust's deferred-pension comparison says a later CV election can involve recalculation. Its historical numerical examples are not current assumptions. CIA section 3520 distinguishes quote validity from recomputation at a new valuation date; section 3550 calls for disclosure of the applicable period.

Read the expiry and response dates on your statement. Ask OPTrust whether a fresh calculation is needed, which date it uses and how interest up to payment is treated. The plan text provides payment-interest rules, but this guide does not promise that a quote stays fixed until payment or prescribe an unverified universal 90-day or six-month window.

OPTrust transfer agreements versus commuted value

A service transfer aims to establish pension credit in a receiving plan. It is not the same transaction as putting a CV into a LIRA. OPTrust participates in MOPPs and bilateral arrangements; its official transfer page lists current participants, including OMERS under MOPPs.

Eligibility and application periods depend on the agreement. The receiving plan evaluates the credit available; a shortfall can mean less credit or an additional payment. Request both plans' written explanations before settling your OPTrust benefit elsewhere. For the other side of that possible move, see our OMERS guide.

Deferred pension versus commuted value

Build your comparison around the retirement income you need and the risks you can bear. A larger lump sum does not establish a better outcome. Consider longevity, investment costs and responsibility, inflation, survivor needs, retirement timing, and any insured benefits. Locked-in money also has access restrictions, even when you control the investments.

Use the general deferred-pension comparison for the broader framework. Keep your OPTrust entitlement and benefits alongside the numbers, and obtain independent financial and tax advice for an irreversible election.

Estimating your OPTrust commuted value

Useful starting information includes your accrued pension, statement date, birth date, pension commencement assumptions, and separate lifetime and temporary/bridge amounts where applicable. Read indexation and survivor details carefully. A salary or contribution balance alone is not enough to describe the benefit.

Before making an election

  1. Confirm your schedule of benefits and check your service, salary and household details on the statement.
  2. Ask OPTrust to confirm CV eligibility, grow-in or divestment provisions, and any direct PSPP transfer implications.
  3. Record the statement response deadline, quote expiry, applicable age cutoff and separate service-transfer deadlines.
  4. Confirm the receiving arrangement and the breakdown between locked-in funds, taxable excess and separate refunds.
  5. Compare retained pension and insured benefits with transfer scenarios; seek advice before signing a final election.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take my OPTrust pension as a lump sum?

Possibly. The ordinary commuted-value option depends on deferred-pension entitlement, election before the applicable age cutoff and other plan conditions. Being under 55 alone is not sufficient. Special small-pension and shortened-life rules differ. Ask OPTrust to confirm the options on your statement.

Can I transfer my OPTrust pension to a LIRA?

An eligible commuted-value transfer can go to a prescribed locked-in arrangement such as a LIRA. Confirm eligibility, the receiving account and the tax-deferred amount with OPTrust before making an election.

Can I take a commuted value after age 55?

The ordinary primary-schedule deferred-pension CV election must be made before 55. Special payments, such as qualifying small-pension or shortened-life payments, have separate rules. A service transfer can also differ from a CV payout. Confirm your circumstances with OPTrust.

Is an OPTrust commuted value taxable?

An eligible direct transfer within the applicable tax limit is tax-deferred. An excess paid in cash is taxable and subject to withholding; RRSP contribution room may allow separate tax relief. Contribution refunds are a separate component. Your statement and individual tax circumstances determine the treatment.

Can I transfer OPTrust service to another pension plan?

Possibly, under an applicable transfer agreement and its eligibility and timing requirements. The receiving plan determines the credit available; it is not necessarily the same number of years or the same amount as a CV paid to a LIRA.

Why did my commuted value change?

A different valuation date, interest assumptions, age or benefit information can change the result. An expired quote or a later election may require recalculation. Ask OPTrust which valuation date and payment adjustments apply.

Can CVCalculator calculate my exact OPTrust transfer value?

No. CVCalculator provides educational estimates and scenarios from your inputs. It does not determine eligibility or reproduce OPTrust's administrative calculation. Your official statement, current plan terms and applicable law govern the election.
