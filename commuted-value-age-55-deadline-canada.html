Commuted value deadlines: when does age 55 matter?

There is no single Canadian age cutoff or election window for transferring a defined-benefit pension. Your plan, retirement eligibility and relevant dates determine your options. Age 55 matters for some plans; it is not a deadline every pension member can rely on.

By CVCalculator · Updated November 15, 2026 · 5 min read

For OPSEU Pension Plan members, age alone does not establish commuted-value eligibility. See the OPTrust eligibility and timing guide for the ordinary deferred-pension route, special cases and the distinction between an election cutoff and quote expiry. Confirm your own deadlines with OPTrust.

Separate eligibility, election and quote dates

Eligibility tells you whether an option is available. The election deadline tells you when to respond. Quote validity tells you how long a stated amount applies before recalculation. Leaving employment and ending plan membership may also occur on different dates.

FSRA's commuted-value guide explains that restrictions depend on the plan. Its Ontario pension life-events guidance distinguishes members eligible for early retirement from those who are not; plan terms can matter for transfers after early-retirement eligibility.

Examples of different plan rules

  • OMERS: retirement eligibility depends on normal retirement age: the early-retirement birthday is 55 for NRA 65, or 50 for NRA 60. Its ordinary CV transfer is a one-time option with an expiry date; small-pension provisions differ. See OMERS' official leaving guidance.
  • Ontario Teachers': the ordinary transfer request must precede immediate-pension eligibility, with the earlier of the month before age 50 or disability-pension approval relevant. Earlier employment histories have different rules. Check Teachers' pension benefit options.
  • HOOPP: the ordinary LIRA or defined-contribution transfer route is for members under 55. A transfer to another defined-benefit plan has different conditions. Read HOOPP's leaving guidance.
  • CAAT: check the membership extension, benefit design and retirement eligibility rather than just your age when you leave work. Our CAAT guide separates these questions.
  • PSPP and OPTrust: age alone does not establish entitlement. See the Ontario PSPP guide and OPTrust guide for the applicable deferred-pension routes and special cases.

OMERS is an Ontario-regulated plan, not a federal pension plan. Do not apply Ontario examples to another province or to a federal plan. The federal public service plan has its own governing statute and options; it is distinct from federally regulated private-sector plans.

Does a late package or missed deadline end every option?

Do not assume either an automatic extension or a permanent loss of every transfer route. Contact your administrator promptly if the package is late, unclear or already overdue. Ask which deadline applies, what happens without a response and whether an eligible deferred member can make a later election. Different plans answer these questions differently.

Questions to ask before deciding

  • Which pension law, benefit design and retirement eligibility apply to me?
  • Which date is used to test my age and service?
  • When must you receive my election and completed transfer documents?
  • When does the quote expire, and when would the amount be recalculated?
  • What pension, transfer or special lump-sum options remain if I do not elect?

Use your official package to decide. An educational estimate can help you prepare questions, but cannot establish transfer entitlement or extend a deadline. Compare the pension decision factors and estimate methodology and limitations.

Frequently asked questions

At what age do you lose the right to take your commuted value in Canada?

There is no universal Canadian cutoff. Your plan, applicable pension rules and retirement eligibility determine whether and when you can transfer. Confirm the relevant age date and election deadline with your administrator.

What happens to my commuted value if I turn 55 before deciding?

It depends on your plan and the dates used to establish eligibility. Do not assume your birthday either cancels or extends the response period on your package. Ask your administrator to confirm both matters in writing.

Can I take my commuted value after age 55 in Canada?

Some ordinary transfer routes are restricted by retirement eligibility, but other transfers or special lump-sum provisions may differ. Your administrator must confirm which options remain available; age alone does not settle the question.

How long do I have to elect my commuted value after leaving my employer?

Use the deadline in your official package and ask whether a later transfer from a deferred pension remains possible. An election deadline, quote validity and the date used for eligibility are separate questions. There is no universal 90-day window.
